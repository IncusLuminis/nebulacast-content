An abstract digital-art collage merging galaxies with a neural network. Half the composition is built from cosmic imagery — a spiral galaxy and swirling star fields, rendered in deep violet, indigo, and warm gold — and the other half dissolves into an abstract neural-network structure: glowing nodes connected by luminous synaptic lines, arranged in loose layers like a diagram, with the connections curving and blending seamlessly into the spiral arms of the galaxy so the two motifs feel like one continuous structure rather than two separate halves stitched together. Faint translucent strings of small numbers, probability-curve shapes, and thin data-plot lines drift through the composition like data streams, some following the neural connections, some tracing orbits within the galaxy. A few bright node-points along the network pulse like stars, and a few stars in the galaxy are subtly rendered as network nodes, blurring the line between the two systems. Mood: abstract, intelligent, luminous — the feeling of a machine quietly learning the shape of the cosmos. Palette: deep indigo-black background, warm gold and violet for the galaxy side, cool cyan and electric blue for the neural-network side, soft white for the node points and data threads. Composition: the galaxy and the neural network occupy opposite diagonal corners, merging smoothly through the center of the frame; generous negative space and a subtle starfield throughout. No readable text, no logos, no UI elements — only abstract numerals and curve shapes as texture, not legible labels.
